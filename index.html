PART I — INTRO

The Extraction of Value in the AI Economy

How does your digital life produce, consume, and redistribute value—and for whom? Every click, post, and creative upload forms an unpaid labor foundation for generative models.

PART II — DATASET EXPLOITATION

Raw Material Without Consent

Generative systems—from ChatGPT to Sora and Gemini—rely on vast scraped datasets of human art, writing, and design. Human expression is transformed into corporate training data without attribution, compensation, or clear legal consent.

PART III — AUTOMATION & DEVALUATION

Devaluing Traditional Craft

By automating tasks in visual design, copywriting, musical composition, and video editing, capital shifts away from traditional craft. Human labor is compressed while platform monopolies capture the surplus value.

PART IV — GLOBAL INEQUALITIES

The Shadow Labor Force

Behind the clean interface of AI lies hidden human labor. Thousands of workers in the Global South perform content moderation, data labeling, and annotation under low wages and high psychological strain.

PART V — CONCLUSION

Restructuring Cultural Production

AI is not merely a technological shift; it is a fundamental restructuring of creative economy. Does it democratize creation, or entrench tech monopolies? The future of human artistic labor hangs in the balance.
