Thesis: Generative AI models (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini, Sora) do not generate value out of thin air; they expropriate centuries of collective human artistic output, automate creative tasks, devalue human work, and rely on hidden, underpaid labor in the Global South to sanitize and calibrate their systems.
