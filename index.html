Digital Value & Political Economy

Generative AI &
Creative Labor

Disrupting economic, cultural, and professional power within digital media industries.

Final Research Presentation
01 / Key Question

How Does My Digital Life
Produce, Consume & Redistribute Value?

Production

Every keystroke, uploads, and artistic outputs are captured as uncompensated training datasets.

Consumption

We access automated media, trading creative agency for speed, convenience, and low friction.

Redistribution

Value shifts away from independent human creators directly toward centralized cloud capital platforms.

02 / Raw Material

Artists' Output as
Uncompensated Input

Generative AI platforms (ChatGPT, Gemini, Sora) rely heavily on vast datasets compiled from human-produced creative works.

  • No Clear Consent: Data scraped without explicit artist opting-in.
  • No Direct Compensation: Zero royalties or financial share for base assets.
  • Zero Transparency: Opaque data sources shielded behind proprietary black boxes.
03 / Capital Consolidation

Siphoning Value:
Creators to Platforms

The economic value historically earned by specialized writers, designers, illustrators, and animators is consolidated into proprietary infrastructure.

"The platform economy turns creative expression into zero-cost raw material, extracting value from labor to build closed commercial monopolies."
04 / De-skilling

Automation Across
Creative Disciplines

Writing Graphic Design Illustration Music Production Video Editing

Automated workflow agents disparage traditional skills built over decades, compressing turnarounds and devaluing craft expertise under the guise of efficiency.

05 / Invisible Labor

The Global South &
Hidden AI Sub-Labor

AI does not build itself. High-tech platforms rely on precarious, low-wage human labor outsourced across the Global South for:

Data Labeling

Categorizing millions of images for minimal piece-rate pay.

Annotation

Refining prompt outputs for reinforcement learning (RLHF).

Content Moderation

Filtering violent and toxic material to keep models safe for consumer launch.

06 / Conclusion

Democratic Expansion or
Reinforced Hierarchy?

Generative AI offers democratized creative tools, but simultaneously drives a fundamental restructuring of cultural production.

Addressing these dynamics requires rethinking ownership, demanding labor transparency, and protecting human agency in the digital economy.
