Midterm Thesis

The Restructuring of Creative Labor

How Generative AI shifts economic, cultural, and professional power within digital media industries.

"How does my digital life produce, consume, and redistribute value, and for whom?"

Overview: Generative AI, Data Extractivism, and Digital Value

Human artistry turned into training data without consent or compensation.

1. Data Extractivism

Artists' output as raw material for tech platforms.

  • Unconsented Ingestion: LLMs and diffusion models rely on massive scrape datasets of human work.
  • Value Extraction: Value moves away from original creators toward platform operators (e.g., OpenAI, Google, Runway).
  • Ethical Crisis: Raises fundamental questions about digital copyright, ownership, and platform exploitation.

2. Disparaging Traditional Skills

Automating tasks across writing, design, music, and video editing.

Writing & Copy

Automating drafts, marketing copy, and news summaries.

Design & Art

Generative image synthesis replacing entry-level illustration work.

Audio & Video

Automated mixing, voice synthesis, and generative video clips (Sora).

"Automation doesn't just increase efficiency; it redefines whose labor is valued and whose skills are deemed obsolete."

Low-wage data annotators and content moderators power modern AI models.

3. Global Inequalities in AI

The invisible human labor behind "automated" intelligence.

  • Outsourced Annotation: Heavy reliance on workers in the Global South for data labeling, RLHF, and moderation.
  • Precarious Conditions: Low-wage, high-stress labor environments necessary to keep models safe and accurate.
  • Embedded Hierarchy: Reinforces colonial-style economic dependencies in the global tech supply chain.

4. Expansion or Hierarchy?

Weighing the dual nature of AI in cultural production.

Democratization & Opportunity

  • Lowers barrier to entry for solo creators and indie studios.
  • Enables rapid prototyping and new cross-medium experimentation.
  • Accelerates routine iterative tasks.

Corporate Consolidation

  • Concentrates capital and infrastructure control in tech monopolies.
  • Devalues domain expertise and creative fair compensation.
  • Homogenizes cultural output based on statistical averages.
Conclusion

A Structural Restructuring of Labor

Generative AI is not merely a technical upgrade—it represents a fundamental restructuring of creative labor and cultural production. Rebalancing this economy requires transparency, fair compensation, and ethical oversight for creators worldwide.
